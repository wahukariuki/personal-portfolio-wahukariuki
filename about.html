About Me

Hello! My name is Abigail Wahu. I am a fourth-year student at Strathmore University studying Computer Science. I have always been curious about how technology can be used to solve real-world problems, and this curiosity has guided my journey in education and personal projects. Over the years, I’ve gained experience through academic projects, hackathons, and personal initiatives that have shaped both my technical and leadership skills.

Skills & Technologies

I have strong skills in programming languages such as Python, Java, and JavaScript. I also enjoy working with frameworks like React and Flask, and I am comfortable with tools like Git, Docker, and Kubernetes. My interests in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning have led me to explore NLP, computer vision, and data visualization. Alongside technical skills, I value teamwork, problem-solving, and clear communication as essential parts of my growth.

Hobbies & Passions

Outside of academics, I love mentoring the youth, public speaking, and exploring new technologies. I’m also passionate about entrepreneurship, especially in creating minimalist multipurpose products that make life easier. In my free time, I enjoy reading, brainstorming innovative ideas, and participating in community activities that bring people together.

Career Goals

My ultimate goal is to build a career at the intersection of AI, innovation, and entrepreneurship. I aspire to create solutions that not only push the boundaries of technology but also make a meaningful social impact. In the future, I would love to lead projects that combine creativity, technology, and purpose-driven design to address challenges in education, sustainability, and personal growth.
